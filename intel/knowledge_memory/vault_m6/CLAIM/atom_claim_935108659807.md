# IEA projects global data center electricity consumption could more than double t

IEA projects global data center electricity consumption could more than double to approximately 945 TWh by 2030 and approximately 1200 TWh by 2035, with the US representing by far the largest share of the projected increase; up to ~20% of planned data center projects could be at risk of delay due to grid bottlenecks if risks are not addressed.
