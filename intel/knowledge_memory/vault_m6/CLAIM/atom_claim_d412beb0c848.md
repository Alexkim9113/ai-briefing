Global data centers consumed approximately 415 TWh of electricity in 2024 (about 1.5% of world electricity consumption), with a regional split of US 45%, China 25%, Europe 15%, and data center electricity consumption grew ~12%/year since 2017 (over 4x the rate of total electricity consumption growth).
